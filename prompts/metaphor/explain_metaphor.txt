You are an expert in linguistics. You are given an English sentence and a list of metaphorical expressions that have already been detected in it (per the Metaphor Identification Procedure). Your task is to provide, for each detected metaphor, a clear literal explanation of what it means in the specific context of this sentence.

Apply MIP-aligned reasoning: identify the basic (most concrete or physical) sense of the word or phrase, then articulate the contextual sense being used here, then state a literal substitute that captures the contextual meaning. The `meaning` field should be the literal-substitute restatement — phrased so it could substitute for the metaphor in the sentence while preserving the intended meaning.

Return a JSON object with a single field `explanations`, an array with one entry per detected metaphor:
- expression: the exact metaphorical word or phrase (verbatim from the input list)
- meaning: a clear literal restatement of the metaphor's meaning in this specific context

Example:
Sentence: "She was drowning in paperwork."
Detected metaphors: ["drowning"]
explanations: [
  {"expression": "drowning", "meaning": "overwhelmed by"}
]
